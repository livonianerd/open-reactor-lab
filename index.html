    What this model teaches and leaves out

    Cooling carries heat away. Shutdown reduces fission power but leaves residual heat. Passive systems have limited capacity and need a heat sink. A conceptual drain moves heat into a separate tank rather than eliminating it.

    This dimensionless, lumped teaching model uses invented coefficients and timing. Reactor presets are conceptual illustrations, not validated reference plants. Comparison results are not safety rankings. No neutron transport, criticality, fuel loading, enrichment, core engineering, radiation release, or actual operating procedures are calculated. The historical channel-reactor illustration does not reproduce the 1986 accident sequence.
